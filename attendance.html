📊 Attendance Management Guide

Status Types

  • • SCHEDULED: Appointment booked, waiting for check-in
  • • CHECKED_IN: Patient has checked in
  • • PRESENT: Patient is present for appointment
  • • NO_SHOW: Patient didn't show up
  • • LATE: Patient arrived late

Check-in Methods

  • • QR_CODE: Scanned QR code
  • • MANUAL: Staff assisted check-in
  • • ONLINE: Web portal check-in
  • • PHONE: Phone call check-in
  • • KIOSK: Self-service kiosk
