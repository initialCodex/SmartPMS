📋 How to Check In

QR Code Scanning

  • • Click "Start Camera" to begin scanning
  • • Hold your QR code in front of the camera
  • • Wait for automatic detection
  • • Check-in will be processed automatically

Manual Entry

  • • Type your QR code in the input field
  • • QR codes start with "ATTEND-"
  • • Click "Check In" to process
  • • Contact reception if you need help
